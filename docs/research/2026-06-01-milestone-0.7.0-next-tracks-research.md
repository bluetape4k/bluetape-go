# 0.7.0 Next Tracks Research

## Milestone

0.7.0 - Research track for graph, text, image, audit, AWS, and I/O helper
packages.

## bluetape4k Evidence

- `bluetape4k-graph`: graph abstractions, backends, graph I/O, and domain
  examples.
- `bluetape4k-text`: tokenizer-core, Korean/Japanese tokenizers, text search,
  and language detection.
- `bluetape4k-image`: image processing and benchmark modules.
- `bluetape4k-javers`: audit, aggregate, event, and history concepts.
- `bluetape4k-aws`: S3, SQS, DynamoDB, Ktor/Spring examples, and SDK helper
  patterns.
- `bluetape4k-projects/io/*`: HTTP, gRPC, CSV, protobuf, Avro, Tink, JSON, and
  codec helpers.
- `bluetape4k-projects/io/tink`: AEAD/DAEAD, associated data, versioned
  keysets, Redis keyset stores, MAC, digest, and rotation guidance.

## Go Direction

0.7.0 is not a delivery bucket. It is the discovery gate that decides which
large domains deserve concrete milestone commitments. Relational SQL, AWS,
text, audit, and graph already have follow-up delivery milestones in 0.8.0
through 0.12.0.

## Decision

Use 0.7.0 to prevent accidental broad rewrites. Each research issue must end
with implement/adopt/example-only/defer recommendations and follow-up issues
only for candidates with Go-specific value.

## Additional utility-boundary research

- #275: Observability and `log/slog` research closes without implementation
  follow-up. Go packages should use package-local explicit hooks, caller-owned
  `log/slog`/OpenTelemetry bridges, and no global logger or MDC-shaped facade.
- #276: Geo and coordinate research closes without implementation follow-up.
  Future consumers must split pure geohash/value work from provider geocoding,
  GeoIP database lookup, projection, and GIS/data packages.
- #277: Statistics and math research closes without implementation follow-up.
  Future consumers must prove data shape, precision, NaN/Inf, weighting, and
  dependency needs before adding Gonum or shared numeric helpers.
